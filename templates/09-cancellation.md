# Cancellation & Refund Policy Templates

Templates for when a guest cancels, requests a refund after cancellation, or you need to cancel on your end.

---

## Template 1: Guest Cancels Within Policy — Full Refund

**When to use:** The guest cancels within your free cancellation window. Straightforward, keep it warm.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I received your cancellation for [dates] at [Property Name]. No worries at all — your full refund will be processed through [platform] and should appear within [5-10 business days, per platform policy].

If your plans change and you'd like to rebook, I'd be happy to host you. Just reach out anytime.

Hope to see you in [area] in the future!

[Your Name]

---

## Template 2: Guest Cancels Outside Policy — Partial or No Refund

**When to use:** The guest cancels after the free cancellation window, and your policy means they receive a partial refund or no refund.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I received your cancellation request for [dates] at [Property Name].

Per the [cancellation policy name — e.g., "Moderate" / "Strict" / "Firm"] cancellation policy on the listing, [explain what applies]:

- **If partial refund:** You're eligible for a refund of [amount or percentage], minus the service fee. That should process through [platform] within [timeframe].
- **If no refund:** Unfortunately, cancellations made within [X days] of check-in aren't eligible for a refund under the policy you agreed to at booking.

I understand plans change, and I'm sorry it didn't work out this time. If there are extenuating circumstances you'd like me to consider, I'm open to discussing it.

[Your Name]

---

## Template 3: Guest Asks for a Refund Exception

**When to use:** The guest cancels outside the window and asks you to override the policy and issue a refund anyway.

**If you're willing to offer something:**

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I understand — these things happen and I appreciate you reaching out directly.

While the cancellation policy on the listing doesn't allow for a full refund at this point, I'd like to work with you. Here's what I can offer:

- [Option A: "A 50% refund if I'm able to rebook the dates"]
- [Option B: "A credit toward a future stay within the next 12 months"]
- [Option C: "If I can rebook the dates with another guest, I'll refund the nights I fill"]

Let me know what works for you, and I'll get it sorted through [platform].

[Your Name]

---

**If you're not willing to override:**

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I appreciate you reaching out, and I'm sorry the timing didn't work out.

Unfortunately, I'm not able to offer a refund outside of the cancellation policy. The [policy name] policy was displayed at the time of booking, and I rely on those terms to manage the property — when dates are held, I turn away other guests who would have booked them.

I wish I could be more flexible here, but I hope you understand. If you'd like to explore options through [platform]'s support team, they may be able to help with their extenuating circumstances process.

I hope we get a chance to host you in the future under better circumstances.

[Your Name]

---

## Template 4: You Need to Cancel on the Guest

**When to use:** An emergency, maintenance issue, or double-booking forces you to cancel. This is serious — handle it carefully.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I'm reaching out with some unfortunate news. Due to [honest, brief reason — e.g., "an unexpected plumbing emergency at the property" / "a personal family emergency"], I'm unable to host your upcoming stay at [Property Name] on [dates].

I sincerely apologize for the disruption to your plans. I know how frustrating this is, especially with a trip already planned.

Here's what I'm doing to help:
- **Full refund:** Your complete refund will be processed immediately through [platform]
- **[If possible:] Alternative options:** [e.g., "I've reached out to a fellow host in the area who may have availability — I'll send you their listing if they confirm" / "I can offer you priority booking for alternative dates at a discounted rate"]

Again, I'm truly sorry about this. If there's anything else I can do to help with your travel plans, please don't hesitate to ask.

[Your Name]

---

## Template 5: Guest No-Show

**When to use:** The guest never arrives and doesn't communicate. Document this for your records and platform protection.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I noticed you didn't check in last night for your reservation at [Property Name] ([dates]). I hope everything is okay.

The property is still available and ready for you if your plans changed and you're arriving later. Just let me know your updated arrival time and I'll make sure everything is set.

If you need to cancel, please let me know so we can discuss options based on the cancellation policy.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

[Your Name]

---

**If no response after 24 hours:**

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Following up on my message yesterday — I haven't heard from you and the property hasn't been accessed. I want to make sure you're aware that the reservation is still active and being held for you.

Per [platform] policy, if I don't hear back by [time/date], the reservation will remain as-is and the standard cancellation policy applies.

Please reach out when you can.

[Your Name]

## Tips

- **Never cancel on a guest unless absolutely necessary.** Host cancellations tank your ranking on every platform and can result in penalties, fines, and calendar blocks.
- **Document everything.** If a guest asks for a policy exception, keep the conversation on-platform so there's a record.
- **Rebook-and-refund is your best compromise.** Offering to refund nights you rebook keeps you whole while being fair to the guest.
- **Know your platform's extenuating circumstances policy.** Airbnb and VRBO both have processes for situations like natural disasters, illness, or government travel restrictions. Point guests there when it applies — it protects both of you.
- **Respond to cancellations quickly.** The sooner you acknowledge, the sooner you can reopen your calendar and potentially rebook the dates.
