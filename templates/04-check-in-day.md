# Check-In Day Templates

Send the morning of check-in as a quick, friendly reminder.

---

## Template 1: Standard Check-In Day Message

**When to use:** Morning of check-in, every booking.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Happy check-in day! Just a quick reminder:

- **Check-in time:** [Time]
- **Address:** [Address]
- **Entry:** [Code / key instructions]

Everything is ready for you. If your plans change or you're running late, no worries — just let me know.

See you soon!

[Your Name]

---

## Template 2: Check-In Day — Early Arrival Request

**When to use:** The guest has asked to check in early, and you can (or can't) accommodate.

**If you can accommodate:**

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Good news — the property is ready, so you're welcome to check in early! You can head over anytime after [early time].

- **Address:** [Address]
- **Entry:** [Code / key instructions]

Enjoy!

[Your Name]

---

**If you can't accommodate:**

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I checked with our cleaning team, and unfortunately we won't be able to get the property ready before the standard [check-in time] today. I want to make sure everything is spotless for you.

If you arrive early, here are a couple of nearby options to pass the time:
- [Coffee shop, restaurant, or activity nearby]
- [Another option]

I'll message you as soon as the property is ready if it's earlier than [check-in time]. Sorry about the inconvenience!

[Your Name]

---

## Template 3: Check-In Day — Late Arrival

**When to use:** The guest has mentioned they'll be arriving late (after dark, late flight, etc.).

---

Hi [Guest Name],

No problem arriving late! The property is self-check-in, so you can get in whenever you arrive.

A few notes for a late arrival:
- **Entry:** [Code / key instructions — repeat from pre-arrival for easy access]
- **Parking:** [Where to park, especially if it's dark and hard to see]
- **Lights:** [How to find the light switch or if there's an exterior light on a timer]
- **Quiet hours start at [time]**, so just a heads-up for the neighbors

Everything you need is inside. Get settled and rest up — if you need anything, I'll respond first thing in the morning.

Safe travels!

[Your Name]

---

## Tips

- **Keep it short.** They already have the full details from your pre-arrival message. This is just a nudge.
- **Automate it.** Set this as a scheduled message on your platform so you never forget.
- **Repeat the entry code.** It's the one thing they'll need in the moment, and scrolling back to find it is frustrating.
