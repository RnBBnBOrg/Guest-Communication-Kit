# Pre-Arrival Templates

Send 3-5 days before check-in with everything the guest needs to arrive smoothly.

---

## Template 1: Standard Pre-Arrival Message

**When to use:** Every booking, 3-5 days before check-in.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Your stay at [Property Name] is coming up! Here's everything you need for a smooth arrival.

---

**CHECK-IN**
- **Date:** [Date]
- **Time:** [Check-in time, e.g., 4:00 PM]
- **Address:** [Full address]
- **Entry:** [Access instructions — lockbox code, smart lock code, key location, etc.]

**PARKING**
[Parking instructions — where to park, permit info, number of spots available]

**WI-FI**
- Network: [Network name]
- Password: [Password]

**HOUSE ESSENTIALS**
- [Thermostat location/instructions]
- [Hot tub or amenity instructions if applicable]
- [Trash/recycling day and location]
- [Any quirks — "the back door sticks a little, just lift and push"]

**HOUSE RULES (quick version)**
- Quiet hours: [e.g., 10 PM - 8 AM]
- No smoking inside
- [Max occupancy]
- [Pet policy]

**CHECKOUT**
- **Date:** [Date]
- **Time:** [Checkout time, e.g., 11:00 AM]
- Checkout instructions are posted in the property — I'll also send a reminder the day before

---

If anything comes up before or during your stay, just message me here. I typically respond within [timeframe].

Have a great trip!

[Your Name]

## Template 2: Pre-Arrival With Directions

**When to use:** Properties in rural areas, mountains, or anywhere GPS can be unreliable.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Almost time! Here's your check-in info for [Property Name].

**GETTING THERE**
The address is [Full address], but here are step-by-step directions since GPS can be [unreliable / tricky] in the area:

1. [Direction step 1, e.g., "Take exit XX from I-70"]
2. [Direction step 2, e.g., "Turn right on Main Street, go 0.5 miles"]
3. [Direction step 3, e.g., "Turn left into Cedar Lodge — we're building X, unit Y"]

**Landmark:** [Something easy to spot, e.g., "Look for the large wooden sign at the entrance"]

[Include all standard check-in details from Template 1]

Safe travels — see you soon!

[Your Name]

## Template 3: Pre-Arrival for Families

**When to use:** When the booking is clearly a family trip (kids mentioned, multiple bedrooms, family-oriented area).

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Getting excited for your family's stay at [Property Name]! Here's everything you need.

[Include all standard check-in details from Template 1]

**FAMILY-FRIENDLY NOTES**
- **Kid-friendly nearby:** [Park, playground, family restaurant, activity]
- **In the property:** [High chair, pack-n-play, board games, streaming services — whatever you offer]
- **Safety note:** [Any relevant info, e.g., "The deck railing is standard height but please supervise little ones near the stairs"]
- **Grocery run:** [Nearest store] is [distance/time] away if you need to stock up on arrival

Let me know if there's anything else I can do to make your family's stay more comfortable!

[Your Name]

---

## Tips

- **Send 3-5 days out**, not the day before. Guests need time to plan, especially for travel logistics.
- **Include the actual code/key info.** Don't make them dig for it. This is the message they'll pull up when they're standing at the door.
- **Format matters.** Use headers and line breaks. A wall of text gets skimmed and missed.
- **Save this as a template in your platform.** Airbnb and VRBO both support scheduled messages — set this one to auto-send.
