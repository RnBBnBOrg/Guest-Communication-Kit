# Post-Stay & Review Request Templates

Follow up after checkout to thank the guest and encourage a review.

---

## Template 1: Standard Post-Stay Thank You

**When to use:** Day of checkout or the next day, after you've confirmed the property is in good shape.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks again for staying at [Property Name] — it was great having you!

I just left you a review on [platform]. If you have a minute, I'd really appreciate one in return. It helps future guests feel confident booking, and it helps me keep improving.

[If the platform supports direct review links: "You can leave a review here: (link)"]

Hope to host you again sometime!

[Your Name]

---

## Template 2: Post-Stay — Returning Guest Offer

**When to use:** Guests you'd genuinely like to host again (great communication, clean property, respectful).

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Just wanted to say thanks for being such a great guest — you're welcome back anytime.

If you're ever in [area] again, message me directly before booking and I'll [offer a returning guest discount / make sure you get first pick on dates / block off your preferred dates].

I left you a great review — if you have a moment to leave one too, I'd appreciate it!

Take care,

[Your Name]

---

## Template 3: Post-Stay — Minor Issue Acknowledgment

**When to use:** Something small went wrong during the stay (a late cleaner, a broken appliance, Wi-Fi went down briefly) and you want to acknowledge it.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks for staying at [Property Name] and for being so understanding about [the issue]. I've already [addressed it — e.g., "had the appliance repaired" / "spoken with the cleaning team" / "upgraded the router"] so future guests won't run into the same thing.

I hope the rest of your stay made up for it. If you feel comfortable leaving a review, I'd really appreciate it — and I completely understand if the [issue] factors in. Your honest feedback helps me improve.

Thanks again, and I hope we get a chance to host you again under better circumstances!

[Your Name]

---

## Tips

- **Leave your review first.** Guests are significantly more likely to leave a review after they see you've already written one.
- **Don't beg.** One ask is enough. If they don't leave a review, let it go.
- **Wait until you've inspected.** Make sure the property is in good shape before sending a warm thank-you. If there's damage, handle that conversation separately (see the Issue Handling templates).
- **Timing matters.** Send within 24 hours of checkout while the stay is still fresh.
