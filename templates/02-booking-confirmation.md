# Booking Confirmation Templates

Send immediately after a guest books to set the tone and confirm next steps.

---

## Template 1: Standard Booking Confirmation

**When to use:** Any new booking. Send within a few hours of the reservation.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks for booking [Property Name] — excited to host you!

**Your stay:**
- Check-in: [Date], [Time]
- Checkout: [Date], [Time]
- Guests: [Number]

I'll send you a detailed check-in message about [3-5 days / 1 week] before your arrival with directions, entry instructions, and everything you need to know.

In the meantime, feel free to reach out if you have any questions or need recommendations for the area.

Looking forward to your visit!

[Your Name]

---

## Template 2: Booking Confirmation — Longer Stay (5+ Nights)

**When to use:** Stays of a week or more where guests may need extra info about the area.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks so much for booking [Property Name] for [number] nights — glad to have you!

**Your stay:**
- Check-in: [Date], [Time]
- Checkout: [Date], [Time]
- Guests: [Number]

Since you'll be here for a bit, a few things that might be useful:
- **Groceries:** [Nearest grocery store and approximate distance]
- **Laundry:** [Washer/dryer in unit, or nearest laundromat]
- **Wi-Fi:** Full details in the check-in message, but yes — strong and reliable

I'll send a complete check-in guide about a week before your arrival. Until then, don't hesitate to reach out with questions or if you'd like local recommendations.

See you soon!

[Your Name]

---

## Template 3: Booking Confirmation — Holiday / Peak Season

**When to use:** Bookings during holidays, festivals, or peak season when parking, traffic, or availability of local services may be different.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks for booking [Property Name] over [holiday/event]! Great time to be in the area.

**Your stay:**
- Check-in: [Date], [Time]
- Checkout: [Date], [Time]

A couple of heads-ups since it'll be [holiday/peak season]:
- **[Traffic / Parking]:** [Relevant tip, e.g., "The roads into town get busy after 10am — arriving earlier makes for a smoother drive"]
- **[Dining]:** [Relevant tip, e.g., "Restaurants book up fast — I'd recommend making reservations in advance"]
- **[Local event info]:** [If applicable, e.g., "The holiday market runs all weekend on Main Street, a 5-minute walk from the property"]

I'll send full check-in details closer to your arrival. Let me know if you have any questions!

[Your Name]

---

## Tips

- **Send the same day** they book. A quick confirmation builds trust immediately.
- **Keep it short.** They just booked — they don't need a novel. Save the details for the pre-arrival message.
- **Set expectations.** Telling them when to expect check-in details reduces "how do I get in?" messages later.
