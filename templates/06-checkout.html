Guest Communication Kit

Checkout Templates

Send the evening before or morning of checkout with clear, simple instructions.

Template 1: Standard Checkout Reminder

When to use: Evening before checkout, every booking.

Hi [Guest Name],

Hope you had a wonderful stay! Just a quick reminder that checkout is tomorrow by [checkout time].

Before you go:

  • [Start the dishwasher / rinse dishes]
  • [Take out trash to the bin at (location)]
  • [Turn off lights and fans]
  • [Lock the door — it auto-locks / use the deadbolt / etc.]
  • [Return keys to (location) if applicable]
  • [Thermostat — leave at (temp) / we'll adjust remotely]

No need to:

  • Strip the beds
  • Wash towels
  • Deep clean anything

That's it — our cleaning team handles the rest.

If you have a moment, I'd really appreciate a review on [Airbnb / VRBO]. It makes a huge difference for small hosts like me.

Thanks for staying at [Property Name]!

[Your Name]

Template 2: Checkout With Late Checkout Offer

When to use: When there's no same-day turnover and you can offer flexibility.

Hi [Guest Name],

Checkout is tomorrow, and since we don't have a guest arriving right after you, you're welcome to take your time — up to [extended time, e.g., 1:00 PM] if that helps.

Before you go:

  • [Same checklist as Template 1]

If you'd like the late checkout, just let me know and it's yours. No extra charge.

Thanks for a great stay!

[Your Name]

Template 3: Checkout — Same-Day Turnover

When to use: When the next guest arrives the same day and timing is tight.

Hi [Guest Name],

Hope you had a great stay! Just a reminder that checkout is by [checkout time] tomorrow. We do have a guest arriving later in the day, so staying close to that time really helps our cleaning team.

Before you go:

  • [Same checklist as Template 1]

If your travel plans change and you need to leave earlier or later, just let me know as soon as possible and I'll do my best to work it out.

Thank you for being a great guest — I hope to host you again!

[Your Name]

Tips

  • Send the night before, not the morning of. Guests shouldn't wake up to a checkout task list.
  • Keep the checklist short. 4-6 items max. If it feels like a chore list, guests resent it.
  • Tell them what they DON'T need to do. It reduces anxiety and prevents guests from doing things incorrectly (like putting sheets in the wrong place).
  • Ask for the review here. This is the natural moment — they're reflecting on the stay.
