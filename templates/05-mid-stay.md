# Mid-Stay Check-In Templates

A brief message during the stay to catch issues early and show you care.

---

## Template 1: Standard Mid-Stay Check-In

**When to use:** Day 2 of a short stay (2-4 nights), or day 3-4 of a longer stay.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Hope you're settling in and enjoying [the area / your stay]! Just checking in to make sure everything at the property is working well.

If anything needs attention or you have questions, don't hesitate to reach out. Happy to help.

Enjoy the rest of your trip!

[Your Name]

---

## Template 2: Mid-Stay for Longer Stays (5+ Nights)

**When to use:** Extended stays where the guest may need supplies restocked or have questions about the area.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Hope you're having a great time so far! Since you're here for a few more days, I just wanted to check:

- Is everything at the property working well?
- Do you need anything restocked (towels, coffee, toiletries)?
- Any questions about the area I can help with?

No need to respond if everything's great — just want to make sure you're all set.

[Your Name]

---

## Template 3: Mid-Stay After a Known Issue

**When to use:** Something happened (maintenance issue, noise complaint, weather event) and you want to follow up.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Just following up on [the issue — e.g., "the hot water situation" / "the noise last night" / "the power outage"]. I wanted to make sure everything is [resolved / back to normal] and that you're comfortable.

[If applicable: "The [repair/fix] has been completed as of [time]. Please let me know if you notice anything else."]

I appreciate your patience with that — I want to make sure the rest of your stay is smooth. Let me know if there's anything else I can do.

[Your Name]

---

## Tips

- **One message is enough.** Don't check in multiple times — it feels intrusive.
- **Make it easy to ignore.** "No need to respond if everything's great" gives them permission not to reply.
- **Time it right.** Send mid-morning or early afternoon, never late at night.
- **This is where you catch 4-star reviews.** A small issue fixed mid-stay becomes a 5-star story. An issue discovered at checkout becomes a complaint.
