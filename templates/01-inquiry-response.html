Guest Communication Kit

Inquiry Response Templates

Use these when a potential guest reaches out with questions before booking.

Template 1: Standard Inquiry Response

When to use: A guest asks about availability, pricing, or general property questions.

Subject: Re: Your inquiry about [Property Name]

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks for reaching out — happy to help!

[Answer their specific question here.]

A few quick highlights about the property:

  • [Key feature 1, e.g., "We're a 5-minute walk from Main Street"]
  • [Key feature 2, e.g., "Free parking in a private lot"]
  • [Key feature 3, e.g., "Full kitchen with coffee maker and essentials stocked"]

The dates you're looking at are available. If you'd like to go ahead and book, you can do so right through the listing — or let me know if you have any other questions.

Looking forward to hosting you!

[Your Name]

Template 2: Inquiry With Special Request

When to use: A guest asks about pet policies, early check-in, extra guests, or other accommodations.

Subject: Re: Your inquiry about [Property Name]

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks for your message!

Regarding your question about [their request]:

If you can accommodate:
Great news — [we can absolutely accommodate that / that's no problem at all]. [Add any relevant details or conditions, e.g., "We do have a $XX pet fee that covers extra cleaning" or "Early check-in is available if there's no guest checking out that morning."]

If you can't accommodate:
I appreciate you asking. Unfortunately, [we aren't able to accommodate that due to (reason)]. [Offer an alternative if possible, e.g., "However, we do have a luggage storage area if you arrive early" or "I'd recommend (nearby option)."]

Happy to answer anything else — the property is a great fit for [reference their trip type: families, couples, ski trips, etc.].

[Your Name]

Template 3: Inquiry From a Guest With No Reviews

When to use: A new guest with no review history inquires. You want to be welcoming but gather a bit more context.

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks for your interest in [Property Name]!

I'd love to host you. Since I see this might be one of your first stays, I just wanted to ask a couple of quick questions so I can make sure the place is a good fit:

  1. What brings you to the area?
  2. How many guests will be in your group?

Nothing formal — I just like to make sure every guest has a great experience. The property is [brief highlight], and I think you'll really enjoy it.

Looking forward to hearing from you!

[Your Name]

Tips

  • Respond within 1 hour if possible. Fast response time directly impacts your search ranking on Airbnb and VRBO.
  • Mirror their tone. If they're casual, be casual. If they're formal, match it.
  • Always answer their actual question first before adding property highlights.
  • Don't oversell. One or two highlights is enough — the listing does the rest.
