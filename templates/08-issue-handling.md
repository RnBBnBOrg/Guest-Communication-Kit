# Issue Handling Templates

Templates for the difficult conversations — noise complaints, damage, refund requests, and rule violations.

---

## Template 1: Noise or Neighbor Complaint

**When to use:** You receive a noise complaint about your guests from neighbors or property management.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I hope you're enjoying your stay. I did receive a note about [noise / activity] at the property [last night / earlier today]. I understand you're on vacation, and I want you to have a great time — just a friendly reminder that quiet hours are [time to time] to keep things smooth with the neighbors.

I appreciate your understanding — let me know if you need anything!

[Your Name]

---

## Template 2: Noise or Rule Violation — Second Notice

**When to use:** A repeat issue or a more serious violation that needs a firmer tone.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I'm following up on our earlier conversation about [noise / the issue]. Unfortunately, I've received another report, and I do need to take it seriously — [the HOA / property management / neighbors] are involved, and continued violations could result in [consequences — fines, early termination of the stay, etc.].

I know this isn't the message you want to get on vacation. I'm not trying to be difficult — I just need your help keeping things within the house rules so we can avoid any further issues.

Please let me know if there's anything going on that I should be aware of. Happy to talk it through.

[Your Name]

---

## Template 3: Damage Reported

**When to use:** You discover damage after checkout (or during a mid-stay inspection).

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Thanks again for your stay at [Property Name]. During our post-checkout inspection, we noticed [describe the damage — be specific and factual, e.g., "a burn mark on the kitchen countertop" or "the bedroom door frame is cracked"].

I've attached [photos / a photo] for reference.

I understand things happen, and I'm not looking to make this a big deal. The cost to repair/replace is approximately $[amount]. I'd like to resolve this directly with you before involving [the platform's resolution center / insurance].

Would you like to discuss? I'm happy to work with you on a fair resolution.

[Your Name]

---

## Template 4: Refund Request — Justified

**When to use:** Something legitimately went wrong (broken AC in summer, plumbing failure, major cleanliness issue) and a partial refund is appropriate.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I'm really sorry about [the issue]. That's not the experience I want any guest to have, and I take full responsibility.

I'd like to offer [a partial refund of $XX / a refund for the affected night(s) / a discount on a future stay] to make it right. I've already [taken steps to fix it — e.g., "called a plumber" / "spoken with the cleaning team" / "replaced the unit"].

I'll process the refund through [platform] today. You don't need to do anything on your end.

Again, I apologize for the inconvenience. I hope the rest of your experience was enjoyable.

[Your Name]

---

## Template 5: Refund Request — Not Justified

**When to use:** A guest requests a refund for something outside your control, a matter of preference, or something that doesn't warrant compensation.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I appreciate you reaching out about [their concern]. I understand that wasn't ideal, and I'm sorry it affected your experience.

After reviewing the situation, [explain why a refund isn't warranted — be factual, not defensive]:
- [e.g., "The weather conditions were unfortunately outside our control"]
- [e.g., "The listing does note that the property is on a shared road, and road noise can vary"]
- [e.g., "The amenity you're referencing was not included in the listing description"]

I always want my guests to have a great stay, and I'm happy to [offer what you can — e.g., "provide a discount on a future booking" / "address any specific concern you had about the property itself"]. However, I'm not able to offer a refund in this case.

If you'd like to discuss further, I'm open to it. I do want to find a resolution that works for both of us.

[Your Name]

---

## Template 6: Unauthorized Party or Max Occupancy Violation

**When to use:** You learn the guest is hosting a party or has exceeded the listed occupancy.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

I've become aware that [the number of guests at the property exceeds the listed maximum of (number) / an event or gathering is taking place at the property]. Per the house rules and the listing agreement, [this isn't permitted / the maximum occupancy is (number)].

I need the property to be brought back into compliance by [specific time or deadline]. This means [extra guests need to leave / the gathering needs to end].

I understand this may be inconvenient, but I have obligations to [the property / the HOA / the neighbors], and I take them seriously. If this can't be resolved, I may need to [involve the platform / end the reservation early per the cancellation policy].

Please let me know how you'd like to proceed.

[Your Name]

---

## Tips

- **Stay factual, not emotional.** Describe what happened, not how it made you feel.
- **Document everything.** Take photos. Screenshot messages. Save timestamps.
- **Offer resolution before escalating.** Most guests respond better to a direct conversation than a platform dispute.
- **Don't delay.** Address issues promptly. Waiting makes them harder to resolve and harder to document.
- **Know your platform's policy.** Airbnb and VRBO have specific resolution processes and timelines. File within the window.
